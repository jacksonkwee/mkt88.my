## Design Specificity

Everything specific to this product is data, not design: the frozen board anatomy
(1st/2nd/3rd, then Special, then Consolation), the lettered A-M boxes, the
deliberate holes, the per-game jackpot rows. The frame was generic - ten
saturated operator header colours over Bootstrap, 17 type styles, radii of
4/10/12/999px. Removing the card tables left a shell any scoreboard could wear.
The owner has since chosen direction J (warm off-white, amber accents, red
jackpot figure; recorded in plans/001) and the shell has been re-skinned to it.

Detector findings at the time: 8 on the home page and 8 on /lotto-4d, from three
antipatterns worth acting on - low contrast (3), cramped padding (1) and the
always-running marquee - plus two long-line warnings, an overused-font warning
(arial, judged a false positive: the system stack renders the CJK names
natively and avoids a font download) and an intermittent React hydration error.

## Priority Issues

- [P0] No live status anywhere. A player cannot tell "still publishing" from
  "deliberately empty" from "yesterday's board". Suggested: $impeccable layout
  (the row), $impeccable clarify (its wording).
- [P1] 266px of pinned chrome before the first number (marquee 39 + header 125 +
  region strip 54 + tile row 141); the card started at y=305 on a 390x844 phone.
- [P1] The phone home page was 1955px tall on the Magnum slide because every
  slide took the height of the tallest card, leaving ~1400px of empty page.
- [P1] Contrast: white on the Nine Lotto orange measured 2.6:1, and #ff0000 on
  #cccccc 2.5:1. Suggested: $impeccable colorize.
- [P1] Horizontal overflow at 390px (404-414px document width), intermittent.
- [P1] The two animations the site owns ignored prefers-reduced-motion.
  Suggested: $impeccable animate.
- [P2] 17 type sizes and weights where the design system names four.
  Suggested: $impeccable typeset.
- [P2] No image on any page carried loading="lazy" (71 images on the home page).
  Suggested: $impeccable optimize.
- [P2] 26 hard-coded hex values, 128 !important declarations and 311 inline
  styles made a restyle hunt through the file. Suggested: $impeccable extract.
- [P2] "----" carries three meanings. Suggested: $impeccable clarify.
- [P2] Every card carries its own 41-option past-date select. Suggested:
  $impeccable distill.
- [P3] 28 dead href="#" links, two date formats on one screen, and the
  live-pending ellipsis at 1.9:1. Suggested: $impeccable polish.

## Persona Red Flags

- Chinese-only reader: rows stay English on every card ("Draw No:", "Partially
  Won"), and the website has no language control (only the app's Settings).
- Malay-only reader: .L-ms exists on /number-history and /draw-schedule but not
  on the result cards, which are the only surface they need.
- One-handed player mid-draw: the 141px game strip is pinned to the top of an
  844px screen, the least reachable band for a thumb.

## Minor Observations

- The home h1 is 20px/500, a weight used nowhere else; /lotto-4d's h1 is sr-only.
- The marquee duplicates its own string for the loop (correctly aria-hidden).
- Cards carried ten different header tints, none of them ours.

## Questions to Consider

- If eleven games are one product, why did each card wear its operator's colour?
- During a 19:00 reveal, what does the screen tell a player that pull-to-refresh
  does not?
- The 17px numbers only fired below 380px, ten pixels under the stated 390px
  design width. Is the design width 380 or 390?

## Status since this critique (recorded 16 Oct 2026, same day)

Shipped and verified: the live status row (P0); the 141px tile row cut to 78px;
the pager height following the active slide; three contrast failures fixed;
horizontal overflow gone from all four pages; the site's own animations now
respect reduce-motion; 68 of 71 images lazy; SEO line length capped at 72ch; the
J palette across shell, cards, footer, tiles and the app's inner screens; and the
brand/link/tint/grey colours are tokens.

Still open: the 41-option per-card date select, the 17-step type scale, the dead
href="#" links, the two date formats, the remaining inline styles on /statistics
and /draw-schedule, and the three meanings of "----" beyond the status row.
One regression is open: Sabah 88's Lotto 6/45, Lotto 5 and Lotto 6 cards render
the captured date (06-09-2026) instead of the live draw - the three cards have no
snapshot entry of their own.
