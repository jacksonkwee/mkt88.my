UPLOAD TO INTERNAL TESTING
--------------------------
1. Test and release > Testing > Internal testing.
2. Create new release.
3. Upload mkt88-4d-v1.9.aab.
4. Release name: 1.9 (10)
5. Save > Review release > Start rollout to Internal testing.
6. Add testers and open the opt-in link on an Android phone.
7. Confirm the logo splash and the first page.

REQUIRED CLOSED TEST (NEW PERSONAL ACCOUNTS)
--------------------------------------------
Google requires a closed test with at least 12 testers who remain opted in
continuously for at least 14 days. After that, open Dashboard and apply for
production access.

PRODUCTION
----------
After production access is approved:
Test and release > Production > Create new release > upload/promote the AAB
> submit for review.

IMPORTANT POLICY NOTE
---------------------
AdSense is for websites. Do not rely on AdSense web ads inside the Android
app. Use AdMob or keep ads off in the app for the first release.

KEEP THE KEYSTORE SAFE
----------------------
Keep mkt88-app/KEYSTORE-INFO.txt private and never share the keystore or
password publicly. The same upload key is needed for every future update.
