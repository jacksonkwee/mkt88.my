STEP 1 - CREATE THE ADMOB APP AND AD UNITS
------------------------------------------
1. Open https://admob.google.com and sign in.
2. Apps > Add app > Android.
3. Choose the option that matches whether the app is already on a store.
4. Enter app name: ???? 4D.
5. Open App settings and copy the App ID. Format: ca-app-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX~NNNNNNNNNN
6. Go to Ad units > Add ad unit > Banner.
7. Enter a name, for example: Home banner.
8. Copy the Banner ad unit ID. Format: ca-app-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX/NNNNNNNNNN
9. Send us these two IDs. We will put them into admob.xml and rebuild the release AAB.

STEP 2 - COMPLETE PLAY CONSOLE ACCOUNT SETUP
--------------------------------------------
1. Open https://play.google.com/console and sign in as the account owner.
2. If a new personal account, complete:
   Home > Verify that you have access to an Android mobile device > View details.
   Install/open the Play Console mobile app, sign in, select the developer account and tap Verify.
3. Personal accounts created after 13/11/2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers
   opted in continuously for at least 14 days before production access.

STEP 3 - CREATE THE PLAY CONSOLE APP
------------------------------------
1. Home > Create app.
2. Default language: English (United States) or Chinese (Simplified).
3. App name: ???? 4D.
4. App or game: App.
5. Free or paid: Free.
6. Contact email: a working support email you can show publicly.
7. Accept the Developer Program Policies and US export declarations.
8. Accept the Play App Signing terms.
9. Click Create app.

Complete every required task:

STEP 7 - INTERNAL TESTING
-------------------------
1. Test and release > Testing > Internal testing.
2. Create new release.
3. Upload the signed .aab built with the real AdMob IDs, not the test-ID build.
4. Release name: 2.2 (13) or newer.
5. Save > Review release > Start rollout to Internal testing.
6. Add testers and install from the opt-in link.
7. Verify: icon, splash logo, website, AdMob banner, History, Favourites, and no AdSense ads inside the app.

STEP 8 - CLOSED TESTING FOR NEW PERSONAL ACCOUNTS
-------------------------------------------------
1. Test and release > Testing > Closed testing.
2. Create a closed test release.
3. Add at least 12 testers.
4. They must remain opted in continuously for at least 14 days.
5. Collect feedback and fix issues during this period.

STEP 9 - APPLY FOR PRODUCTION ACCESS
------------------------------------
1. After the closed-test requirement is met, open Dashboard.
2. Click Apply for production.
3. Answer the questions about the closed test, the app and production readiness.
4. Google reviews the application. Review usually takes seven days or less, but can take longer.

STEP 10 - PRODUCTION RELEASE
----------------------------
1. After production access is approved, go to Test and release > Production.
2. Create a new release.
3. Upload/promote the signed .aab with the real AdMob IDs.
4. Add release notes.
5. Review and start rollout.
6. Monitor Policy status, crashes, reviews and AdMob revenue.

----------------------
